    Overview

    A Simple Cookie Approach

    The NEXORA website uses limited browser storage to support normal functionality and remember whether the cookie notice has already been acknowledged.

    Cookies and similar technologies are small pieces of information stored or accessed through your browser when you use a website.

    Their purpose can vary. Some support essential functionality, while others may be used for analytics, personalisation or advertising on websites that implement those technologies.

    Basics

    What Are Cookies?

    Cookies are small data files that websites can ask a browser to store on a device. Similar functionality may also be provided through browser technologies such as local storage.

    These technologies can help a website remember choices between page visits without requiring the visitor to repeat the same action every time.

    Essential

    Supports core website behaviour and basic visitor preferences.

    Preference

    Allows a browser to remember a choice made during a previous visit.

    Optional

    Some websites may use additional technologies for analytics or other optional purposes.

    Current Website

    Essential Browser Storage

    The current website stores a simple browser preference after the visitor accepts the compact cookie notice.

    This prevents the same notice from unnecessarily appearing again during later visits from the same browser where that storage remains available.

    Choice

    Your Cookie Preference

    When the cookie notice appears, you can read this page before acknowledging it. The notice is deliberately compact and does not block normal navigation around the website.

    Once acknowledged, the website stores the preference in the browser so that the notice does not need to keep appearing.

    If browser storage is cleared, blocked or unavailable, the notice may appear again because the previous preference can no longer be read.

    Third Parties

    External Technologies

    Some website resources may be delivered through third-party infrastructure, such as externally hosted fonts or technical services.

    External providers operate under their own technical and privacy practices. If additional analytics, advertising or optional tracking technologies are introduced in the future, this Cookie Policy should be updated to describe their use.

    No hidden marketing claim

    This page does not describe advertising or analytics cookies as active unless they are actually implemented on the website.

    Controls

    Browser Settings

    Most modern browsers provide controls for cookies, site data and local storage. These controls can normally be accessed through the browser's privacy or site settings.

    • Review stored website data.
    • Remove stored preferences.
    • Clear site data for individual websites.
    • Restrict certain types of browser storage.
    • Change broader browser privacy settings.

    Restricting essential browser storage may cause some website preferences to stop being remembered.
